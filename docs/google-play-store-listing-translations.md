# Google Play Store listing translations

## English (en-US)

### App name

MonsterDialer

### Short description

A retro monster battle for every phone call.

### Full description

MonsterDialer turns your phone calls into a retro monster-battle experience.

Make or receive a call and meet a character encounter on your call screen. Set up MonsterDialer as your default phone app, choose your player character, and customize how contacts appear during calls.

Features:

- Retro battle moments for incoming and outgoing calls
- Contact-specific character assignments
- A local collection of characters, unlocks, and battle progress
- Custom character and character-pack imports
- Optional private backup of unlockable variants with your Google account
- Optional online profiles that you can publish and share with a link
- A full phone app with contacts, calling, and call-history features

MonsterDialer works offline for its core phone and battle features. Google Sign-In and online profiles are optional. Online profiles publish only the trainer, monster, and artwork you choose; your phone number, contacts, and call history are not uploaded as part of an online profile.

MonsterDialer is open source and built on OpenDialer.
